# Lectures on Modern Western Thought

Welcome! This digital garden is a personal repository for my notes, breakdowns, and reflections on **Liu Qing's (刘擎)** _Lectures on Modern Western Thought_ (《西方现代思想讲义》).

Lecture on Modern Western Thought is a introductory piece on Western thinkers of the 20th century. It introduces and analyses 19 scholars and their literature on philosophy, sociology, psychology, politics, and more.

I am documenting notes on this book to create a reliable place to look back on some of the informations and takeaways I got from this book.

---

## Core Themes

Before diving into individual scholars, the book establishes fundamental modern dilemmas:

- [[On Thoughts and Reality]] — Why ideas aren't just reflections of the world, but the blueprints that build it.
- [[The Modern Condition]] — Navigating freedom, choice, and the loss of absolute certainties.
